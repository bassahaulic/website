Builder's Guide: Screw Selection

Lamination (Stacking Layers): When gluing double/triple baffles (3/4"), use #8 x 1-1/4" screws. This length bites into the second layer without poking through the face.

Joint Assembly (Corners): When attaching panel edges (90° joints), use #8 x 1-3/4" screws for maximum structural hold.
