🌌 Night Sky

A pocket planetarium for the two of you.

  1. 📍 Set your location so the sky matches what’s above you.
  2. 🧭 Point-at-sky mode — hold your phone up and aim it at the sky; the map follows where you point.
  3. Tap any star, planet or the Moon to identify it.
  4. 🔴 Night mode turns everything red to protect your dark-adapted eyes.
  5. ✨ Tonight shows sunset, darkness, moonrise and which planets are out.

Tip: install it — open your browser menu and choose “Add to Home screen”. Works offline afterwards.

Compass note: phone compasses can drift a few degrees (wave your phone in a figure-8 to calibrate) and point to magnetic north.
